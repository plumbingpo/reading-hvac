Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Reading HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Reading HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Reading and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever the cause, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and document the entire call for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so the same failure doesn't return.
